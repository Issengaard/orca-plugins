Convo Settings

All of Convo Mode's settings live in its own section of Orca Settings → Plugins. Orca draws the list from the plugin's manifest: each setting is text, a number, an on/off switch or a picker, up to 64, and there are no section headers or custom controls. The defaults below are your picks from every round. Change anything to see the "Saved" note Orca gives.
