Convo Mode

One quiet conversation, like your reference: words darken as the agent speaks them, a glow rises while it talks, and the bar at the bottom says who's speaking. Click the title to switch agents or start Orchestrator. The icon at the top left opens the list of agents that need you. Press the mic to play the next scripted turn; on ur-email-drafts, the agent shows a mockup in the conversation, and Open beside puts it next to the chat.

The tab takes its colors from your Orca theme. Orca hands every plugin tab its theme as --orca-* color variables and updates them when you switch, so Convo follows along. Try it with the themes you have installed:

Mockup with Convo still on: press the button below, or Full view on any mockup card. The mockup fills the tab and the conversation shrinks to a pill that keeps listening, speaks with live captions, and updates the mockup as you talk. Switch to the claude · main tab and Convo stays on as a pill in the status bar.
